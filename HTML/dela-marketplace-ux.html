Dela
safe transactions

User flowUI/UXBuyer–sellerBusiness analysis

Dela is an Australia-based buyer and seller platform designed to make online transactions safer, more transparent and stress-free. I designed the complete UI/UX for a system where sellers initiate requests, buyers securely confirm purchases, and payments and deliveries are tracked end-to-end in the app.

Client
DelaExchange, Australia
Services
UX Business Analysis
Industry
E-commerce
Date
March 2025
01

Challenge

Online marketplaces run on uncertainty — dodgy sellers, flaky buyers and deals that fall apart.

The challenge was to remove fear from both sides of the transaction while keeping the flow simple, fast and intuitive.

I prioritised trust-building moments — payment holding, delivery tracking and request confirmations — over secondary features, and researched peer-to-peer marketplaces, escrow-based payment models and user complaints about scams and failed deals.

  • 01Building trust without face-to-face interaction
  • 02Preventing ghosting during transactions
  • 03Making every step clear for both buyers and sellers
02

Solution

A confidence-first UI that supports secure buyer–seller interactions from request to delivery.

As the UI/UX Business Analyst, I structured the experience so sellers can easily send deal requests while buyers keep control through clear confirmations and protected payments.

Payments are held safely in the platform, delivery status is tracked in-app, and every step is communicated transparently. Clear calls-to-action, status indicators and simple language reduce stress and decision fatigue — so users can buy smarter, sell safer and complete transactions with confidence.
