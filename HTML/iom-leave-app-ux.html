IOM World
smart leave app

UI/UXMobileLeave requestsGeofenced face recognition

An internal workforce solution for IOM World Sri Lanka that streamlines leave applications, overtime (OT) tracking and attendance — delivered as a smart, secure and user-friendly system with AI-powered geo-based face recognition and automated approval workflows.

Client
IOM World Singapore
Services
UX Business Analysis
Industry
HR / Workforce management
Date
March 2025
01

Challenge

Manual leave, overtime and attendance created delays, inefficiencies and inconsistent data.

The system had to let employees apply for leave and OT seamlessly while staying accurate, secure and compliant with organisational policy.

Research into enterprise HR systems, AI-based attendance solutions and pain points in the existing manual workflows helped prioritise features that reduce administrative effort while improving transparency and trust.

  • 01Geo-based face recognition for attendance verification
  • 02Different leave types and approval hierarchies
  • 03Easy to use for non-technical users
  • 04Time sensitivity, data privacy and real-time validation
02

Solution

A smart leave and workforce platform with minimal friction for employees and clear approval controls for managers.

I was responsible for the complete UI/UX — intuitive flows for leave requests, OT submissions, approval tracking and notifications — and worked closely with the team to integrate AI-powered geo-location and face recognition to validate attendance.

Beyond design, I contributed to development by implementing front-end components and aligning the system with the selected theme for a consistent, professional experience.

(Client’s feedback)

The leave management system delivered by the team has greatly improved how we manage attendance, leave, and overtime. The UI is clear and easy to use, and the AI-based face recognition with geo-location adds a strong layer of security.
Mr JitendraOperations Manager, IOM World
