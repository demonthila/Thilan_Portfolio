A custom project management system for Novalink Innovations, designed and developed to improve project tracking, notifications and planning — with streamlined workflows, AI-assisted planning and real-time email notifications.

Client
Novalink Innovations (Internal)
Services
UI/UX Design, Front-end Development, AI Integration
Industry
Project management / SaaS
Date
2026
01

Challenge

Handle complex project management features without clutter or cognitive overload.

Novalink needed a system to manage internal projects while keeping every team member updated with real-time notifications and AI-assisted planning suggestions.

I mapped key user workflows, prioritised high-frequency actions such as task creation, project tracking and notification management, and researched modern project management tools.

  • 01A fast, responsive and scalable SPA interface
  • 02AI-based planning for task prioritisation and scheduling
  • 03Automated email notifications for updates and deadlines
  • 04Dashboards with quick insight into project progress
  • 05Consistency across many modules and features
02

Solution

A single-page application that balances full functionality with usability.

The system reduces manual planning effort, improves team awareness through notifications and delivers a modern UI tailored to internal SaaS needs.

  • 01Built with React 18, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS
  • 02Modular UI components for consistency across screens
  • 03AI-assisted planning for smarter scheduling and forecasting
  • 04Real-time email notifications for assignments, deadlines and updates
  • 05Dashboards for high-priority tasks, timelines and upcoming deadlines

They took the time to understand our complex project management requirements and translated them into a highly efficient and visually stunning system. The AI integration has already saved our team hours of manual planning.
