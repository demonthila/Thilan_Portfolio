Redesigns, brands and experiments that didn't need a full case study, with notes on the decisions behind them.

Web · HR system · 2024

OrangeHRM redesign

A redesign of a feature-rich HR system for leave, attendance and employee records.

The question behind it

Could a dense HR tool feel quick for everyday tasks?

Inside the idea

I focused on the three most-used workflows: leave, records and attendance. Clearer hierarchy, shorter forms and the team calendar beside the leave form.

The design lesson

In an enterprise redesign, respecting what users already know matters as much as improving it.

Brand identity · 2025

NextStop identity

Logo, orange brand pattern and staff ID badge for NextStop.

The question behind it

How does a brand stay recognisable on a badge and a screen?

Inside the idea

A simple mark with a repeatable pattern, so the identity works at any size and on any surface.

The design lesson

A good identity system is mostly rules: colour, spacing and where the mark goes.

Brand graphics · 2025

Pet Mate graphics

A hanging sign and community welcome banner for Pet Mate.

The question behind it

What makes a local brand feel friendly from across the street?

Inside the idea

Warm colour, rounded shapes and short, readable lines of text.

The design lesson

Signage is read in seconds. Fewer words, bigger shapes.

Illustration · Dashboard graphics · IOM World

Workforce platform graphics

Illustrations and dashboard graphics for an AI attendance platform.

The question behind it

How do you explain face recognition without a manual?

Inside the idea

Clean illustrations for location and face checks, plus status icons and chart graphics for manager dashboards.

The design lesson

Graphics in a product are part of the UX. They should explain, not decorate.
